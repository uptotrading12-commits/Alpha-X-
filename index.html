Before you confirm

Three numbers to check on every order

Spread

You pay it on entry

On EUR/USD at 1.2 pips, a 10,000 EUR position costs about 1.20 USD to open (10,000 × 0.00012). The price has to move that far before you break even.

Stop-loss

Risk is size times distance

A 10,000 USD position with a stop 0.5% away risks about 50 USD. Change the stop on the ticket and watch “Loss if stop is hit” update.

Margin

Margin is not your maximum loss

At 1:10, 1,000 USD of margin holds a 10,000 USD position. Gains and losses count on the full 10,000, so a fast move can cost more than the margin you put up.

Questions

Before you start

Can I practice before using real money?

Yes. Paper mode gives you a virtual balance and the same order ticket as a live account. Fills are simulated, so real market conditions such as slippage can differ.

What does leverage do?

Leverage lets you hold a position larger than the cash you put up. At 1:10, $1,000 of margin controls $10,000. Gains and losses are both calculated on the full $10,000, so losses can be larger than your margin.

What happens if my margin runs low?

You get a warning as your free margin shrinks. If it falls below the stop-out level, open positions can be closed automatically, starting with the largest loss.

How do stop-loss and take-profit work?

A stop-loss closes your position when the price reaches the level you set, which caps the loss you planned for. In fast markets the fill can be worse than the stop, so it limits risk without removing it. A take-profit closes the position at your target.

Which markets can I trade?

Currency pairs, stock indices, metals, crypto and shares. What you can trade depends on the country you live in.

Risk warning. Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of loss and is not suitable for everyone. You can lose more than you deposit. Prices on this page are simulated, and past performance does not predict future results. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and consider getting independent financial advice.

How do I deposit or withdraw?

Open Wallet from the bottom bar or tap Deposit / Withdraw on the Accounts page. Demo money only; real funding is not live.

How do I move money between accounts?

Tap Transfer on the Accounts page, pick the two accounts and an amount.

Where is my trade history?

Accounts page, then Closed. Summary shows profit, loss and order counts for a day, week, month, year or all time.

How do I change theme or language?

Settings has Light, Dark and System theme, and a Language list.

What are spread and leverage?

Spread is the gap between Buy and Sell price. Leverage lets a small margin control a larger position, so losses grow too.
